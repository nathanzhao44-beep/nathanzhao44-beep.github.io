The VALORANT Arsenal

VALORANT has a wide variety of weapons that players can purchase during the buy phase. Each weapon has different strengths, weaknesses and costs.

Weapons are divided into categories including sidearms, SMGs, shotguns, rifles, snipers and heavy weapons. Choosing the right weapon can be an important part of a team's strategy.
