What Are Agents?

Agents are the playable characters in VALORANT. Each agent has their own abilities and playstyle. Players can choose an agent that suits the way they like to play.

Agents are separated into four main roles: Duelist, Initiator, Controller and Sentinel. Each role has a different purpose and helps the team in a different way.

Agent Roles

Duelists

Duelists are designed to take fights and create openings for their team. Examples include Jett, Phoenix and Raze.

Controllers

Controllers control areas of the map using abilities that can block vision and make it harder for enemies to defend.

Initiators

Initiators help their team enter areas by gathering information and forcing enemies away from important positions.

Sentinels

Sentinels focus on defence, protecting areas and watching for enemies attempting to attack from unexpected angles.
