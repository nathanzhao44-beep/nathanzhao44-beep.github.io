Welcome to VALORANT

VALORANT is a competitive 5v5 tactical first-person shooter developed by Riot Games. Players choose from a variety of agents, each with unique abilities that can be used to create different strategies.

The game combines accurate gunplay with character abilities. Players need to communicate with their teammates, manage their economy and make smart decisions to win rounds.

What Makes VALORANT Different?

One of the most important parts of VALORANT is the combination of weapons and abilities. A player might use an ability to gather information, block an area or help their teammates enter a site.

Teamwork is also extremely important. Players have different roles and abilities, so successful teams need to work together instead of relying only on individual skill.
